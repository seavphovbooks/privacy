My Book – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the My Book app for Android (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles, why, and what choices you have. My Book is an e-reader: it lets you open and read EPUB and PDF files that you choose to add to your device.

The short version

  • No account is needed, and we do not sell your personal information.
  • Your books, reading progress, bookmarks and collections stay on your device.
  • We use Google Firebase for anonymous usage and performance statistics and to receive messages you send us.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising ID.
  • If you add books from GitHub, your phone connects to GitHub. Any access token you save is encrypted on your device and sent only to GitHub.

1. Information that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • the books you add (copies of the EPUB/PDF files, and their covers and titles);
  • your reading progress, bookmarks and collections;
  • your settings (language, appearance, reading display options);
  • GitHub access tokens you choose to save for private repositories (see section 4).

Removing a book, or uninstalling the app, deletes this data from your device.

2. Information we collect

WhatDetailsWhere / who
Messages you send (Settings → Contact Developer) The text you write, the type you pick (feature request, problem report or contact), the app version, your app language, your platform (Android) and the time sent. We do not ask for your name or email address, so please do not include personal details in your message. Stored in Google Cloud Firestore, in our Firebase project.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or the contents of your books. The app’s only Android permission is Internet. It does not request access to your storage, camera, microphone or location: files you add are chosen through Android’s own file picker.

3. Advertising

My Book shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner ads and native ads). To show and measure ads, and to limit fraud, AdMob and Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions. This can include showing personalised ads.

You can control this on your phone: open Settings → Google → Ads (the path varies by device) to reset your advertising ID or to opt out of ad personalisation. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. GitHub and other network requests

When you use Add from Repo, your phone connects directly to GitHub (api.github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com) to list and download the books in the repository you enter. GitHub can see your IP address and the repository you request, under GitHub’s own privacy statement. We do not receive this information.

Private repositories and access tokens

If a repository is private, the app may ask for a GitHub personal access token. A token you enter is saved only on your device, in Android’s encrypted storage, so you do not have to enter it again. It is sent only to GitHub’s API to read the repository, never to us or to anyone else. You can delete saved tokens at any time from the lock icon on the Add from repo screen. We recommend creating a token with read-only access to a single repository.

5. How we use information

  • to run and maintain the app;
  • to read and reply to the messages you send us, and to fix problems;
  • to understand which features are used and to improve the app and its performance;
  • to show ads, which helps keep the app available.

We do not use this information to identify you personally, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. Information is processed on our behalf by service providers, mainly Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring, Cloud Firestore and AdMob). Their handling of data is described in the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. Data may be processed on Google servers in countries other than your own. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you remove it or uninstall the app.
  • Messages sent to us: kept until we no longer need them to respond or improve the app, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Analytics and performance data: kept by Google for the period set in our Firebase project (up to 14 months by default).
  • Advertising data: kept by Google according to its own policies.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your local data at any time by removing books or uninstalling the app.
  • Ask us to delete a message you sent by contacting us (section 13). Because we do not know who a message is from, please quote some of its text so we can find it.
  • Reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings (section 3).
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise them; we will respond as required by law.

9. Children

My Book is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data sent to Firebase, AdMob and GitHub travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Saved GitHub tokens are protected by Android’s encrypted storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your books and copyright

My Book is a reading tool. It does not provide, sell, host or share any books. Every book you add, from your phone, from a GitHub repository or any other source, comes from you, and you are responsible for making sure you have the right to own, copy and read it. See the Terms & Conditions in the app (Settings → Terms & Conditions).

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available in the app (Settings → Privacy Policy) and at the address where you found this page, and the “Last updated” date above will change.

13. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, use Settings → Contact Developer in the app, or email us at sovathanalun@gmail.com.
